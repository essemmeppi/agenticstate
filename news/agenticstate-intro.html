What is The Agentic State? Let us introduce ourselves

01.10.25

The world is at a turning point. Agentic AI — systems capable of reasoning, acting, and learning across tasks — is rapidly moving from experimental prototypes to everyday tools. While private companies are already rethinking their business models around this technology, governments risk falling behind once again. Yet the stakes for the public sector are higher than ever: the way states adopt agentic AI will shape the future of governance, public trust, and service delivery for decades to come.

This is where The Agentic State comes in.

The Agentic State is an action platform endorsed by governments, research institutions, and international partners. We exist to ensure that governments do not just watch this technological transformation from the sidelines, but lead it in ways that are responsible, ambitious, and oriented toward public value. Our mission is simple: to turn the potential of agentic AI into government action.

We structure our work around what we call the three waves of adoption:

  • Agenda Setting — Building the political and strategic momentum for governments to engage seriously with agentic AI.
  • Moving Theory to Practice — Providing leaders with frameworks, functional areas, and peer networks that help them bridge vision and execution.
  • Implementation — Ensuring governments are prepared to deploy agentic systems responsibly, with the right infrastructure, institutional preconditions, and safeguards in place.

The Agentic State is not a vendor. We do not sell software. We are not a think tank producing reports for the shelf. Instead, we are an action platform: a trusted space where governments, researchers, and international organisations come together to learn, align, and act.

Already, governments in Europe, Latin America, Africa, and Asia are working with us to explore how agentic AI can be applied to compliance systems, citizen-facing services, back-office automation, and digital public infrastructure. By convening these efforts under one platform, The Agentic State creates something no single country could achieve alone: a global community of practice committed to the responsible adoption of agentic AI.

This is just the beginning. Over the coming months, we will release new research, convene new partnerships, and continue shaping the global conversation on agentic government. The Agentic State is here to make sure that when agentic AI transforms how governments operate — it does so in the public interest.
