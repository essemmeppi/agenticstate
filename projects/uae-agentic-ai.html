Agentic AI for the UAE Federal Government

10.06.2026

The United Arab Emirates is moving on agentic AI at a pace few governments match.

The Agentic State team was in the UAE working with the federal government on what that speed makes possible. Luukas Ilves delivered a keynote on building the Agentic State, and Manuel Kilian led a hands-on agentic AI workshop convening 50 federal entities, organised with the Ministry of Cabinet Affairs.

Read the official press release, and the first-hand accounts from Luukas and Manuel on LinkedIn.
