The Agentic State at the US State AI Officers Summit

10.07.2026

In June 2026, chief AI officers and technology leaders from 34 US states, territories, and tribal nations gathered in Atlanta for the State AI Officers Summit, convened by the Center for Civic Futures.

The conversation moved decisively beyond chatbots: how do governments prepare for AI systems that act?

The Agentic State was part of that conversation — our framework was referenced in the summit’s takeaways as governments work through what agentic AI means for procurement, workforce, and governance.

Read the Center for Civic Futures’ write-up, “Building the Government We Need for What’s Next”.
