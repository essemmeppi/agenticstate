LawLab: our Agentic Watchdog for Legislation, featured on WIRED

09.07.2026

What does the Agentic State look like in practice? In Estonia, our co-author Luukas Ilves built LawLab, an AI system that reads every draft bill before the Riigikogu, Estonia’s parliament, and hunts for the kind of errors that usually surface only after a law is passed — when they are expensive to fix. The project was inspired by exactly such a case: a drafting mistake that ended up costing an estimated €28 million.

LawLab works as an automated analysis pipeline: it ingests each bill directly from the parliament’s public data, runs the full text through multiple AI analysis passes, and flags legal and drafting problems — inconsistencies, conflicting provisions, unclear definitions — classifying every finding as high, medium, or low risk. Results are published openly, updated continuously as bills move through proceedings, and available through a public API, so anyone can scrutinise both the legislation and the tool itself. The methodology, including prompts and known limitations, is documented in the open.

WIRED covered the project in “The $28 Million Mistake That Inspired Estonia’s AI F*ckup Finder”. LawLab is giving us a glimpse into the future of legislative oversight in the Agentic State.
