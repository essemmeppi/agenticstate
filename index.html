Who we are

The Agentic State is a global action platform advancing the adoption of agentic AI across governments. Endorsed by governments, tech companies and research institutions, we enable leaders to translate the promise of agentic AI into practice, connecting peers and providing frameworks for action.

Governments consistently lag behind the private sector in adopting new technologies. With agentic AI, the cost of delay is too high. Our mission is to dramatically reduce the government technology gap by enabling responsible and ambitious adoption of agentic AI.
