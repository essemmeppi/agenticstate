Rethinking Government for the Era of Agentic AI

Read our Vision Paper

Initiated and supported by

Agentic AI is set to transform government faster than any technology before it. That’s why acting now is essential. The Agentic State is a global action platform helping public leaders move ambitiously — turning agentic AI into better services, faster decisions and more accountable institutions, while ensuring this shift is guided by public purpose.

Our mission is simple: enable governments to lead in the responsible use of agentic AI for public impact. We see the state as both adopter and enabler — and we work to ensure countries at every stage of digital development can take advantage of the opportunities of agentic AI.

Vision Paper

Launched at the Tallinn Digital Summit 2025, the Vision Paper maps how agentic AI systems will rewire the core functions of government — spanning public services, crisis management, governance, and procurement — and what technological, organisation, and leadership changes are needed to enable this shift.
