Our Work

We prepare public institutions for transformative AI through three lines of work. Research develops the concepts and tools governments need to use AI without losing control of it. Government engagements put that work into practice with public institutions adopting and regulating AI agents. Field building makes institutional preparedness a priority for public leaders, and connects them with each other and with frontier researchers.
